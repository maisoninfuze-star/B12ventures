FAQ

Good To Know.

What areas do you cover?

We shoot across Greater Montréal and most of Québec, and we serve select markets in Morocco. Travel outside the core area may add a small fee — just ask.

How fast do I get the files?

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from the shoot: edited photos, drone video and your 3D tour link, all delivered digitally and ready for MLS / Centris.

Is $300 really per listing?

Yes — one flat rate per property covers the drone video, professional photos and the immersive 3D virtual visit. No subscriptions, no hidden fees. Teams and brokerages doing volume get custom pricing.

Do I need to be there for the shoot?

Not necessarily — as long as we have access to the property. We coordinate timing with you or the seller and handle the rest on-site.

Who owns the photos and video?

You do. Every file is handed over to you with full rights to use across MLS, your website, and social media — no watermarks, no lock-in.
